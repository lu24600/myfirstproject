Digital Technologies Learning Areas

Digital Technologies is a learning area that focuses on developing students' skills and knowledge in the field of technology. It encompasses various aspects of digital literacy, including coding, programming, computational thinking, and digital citizenship. The learning area aims to equip students with the necessary skills to navigate and thrive in a digital world, fostering creativity, problem-solving abilities, and critical thinking skills.Digital Technologies is a learning area that focuses on developing students' skills and knowledge in the field of technology. Digital Technologies and Hangarau Matihiko 1.4

Develop a digital media outcome involve:

  • Using appropriate tools, techniques and design elements for the purpose and end users.
  • Applying appropriate data integrity and testing procedures in the development of the outcome.
  • Describing relevant implications.

Develop an informed digital media outcome involves:

  • Using information from testing procedures to improve the quality and functionality of the outcome.
  • Addressing relevant implications.

Use advanced techniques to develop a refined digital media outcome involves:

  • Iterative improvement throughout the design, development and testing process.
  • Applying design elements effectively.
